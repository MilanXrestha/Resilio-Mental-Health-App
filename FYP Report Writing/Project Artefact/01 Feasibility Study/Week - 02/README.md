## Key Requirements Extracted From Interviews

**From Ms. Sushana Karki:**
- Real licensed therapists only — not AI bots
- eSewa payment (no credit card)
- Works offline (unreliable university WiFi)
- Private entry — no social stigma

**From Dr. Dibyandra Singh:**
- Patient PHQ-9/GAD-7 scores visible in consultation
- Patient mood log visible during session
- Admin verification of therapist credentials
- Crisis protocol for PHQ-9 ≥ 20

## Notable Quotes

> "An app where I can book a session privately and pay with eSewa would completely remove both of those barriers." — Ms. Sushana Karki

> "If I can see a patient's PHQ-9 score and their mood log for the past two weeks before a session, I can spend the first ten minutes doing actual therapy." — Dr. Dibyandra Singh
